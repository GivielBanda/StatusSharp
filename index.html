What is StatusSharp?

StatusSharp is an Android app project designed to help users prepare their media before sharing it on WhatsApp Status.

Our goal is to make sharing your favourite moments simpler while preserving as much visual detail as possible.

Results depend on the original media and the compression applied by the platform you share to.
