# StatusSharp Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 13, 2026** ## Introduction StatusSharp is an Android application designed to help users prepare photos and videos for sharing on social media and messaging platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how StatusSharp handles information when you use the application. ## Information We Collect StatusSharp is designed to process media selected by users. We do not intentionally collect personal information through the application unless a future feature clearly explains otherwise. ## Photos and Videos When you select a photo or video, the application uses that media to provide its intended processing features. Media may be processed on your device. Do not assume that every file is processed exclusively on your device unless the app's actual implementation confirms this. ## Sharing If you choose to share processed media through another application, that application's own privacy policy and practices will apply to your use of it. ## Data Storage and Security StatusSharp aims to handle user media responsibly. The exact storage and retention of files depend on the application's implemented features and your device settings. ## Third-Party Services StatusSharp may be used alongside third-party applications, including WhatsApp. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. ## Children's Privacy StatusSharp is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. ## Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as StatusSharp develops. Any updated version will be published on this page. ## Contact For privacy-related questions about StatusSharp, please contact the app developer through the developer's available contact details.
